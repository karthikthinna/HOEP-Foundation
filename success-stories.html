The Kind of Change We Want to Make

HOEP Foundation is newly founded, and our first programs are still ahead of us. This page shows the kind of support we plan to fund through trusted local partner organizations. As real programs are completed, their stories will be published here, with the permission of the people involved.

Example 1

Elderly Care and Comfort

Community Impact

Many elderly people in care homes live without basic comforts. Support of this kind would provide essentials such as proper bedding and daily care items to a home caring for abandoned seniors, restoring comfort and dignity.

This is an example of the kind of initiative HOEP plans to fund. It is not a completed HOEP project.

Special Needs Education and Support

Inclusive Education & Support

Children with special needs often lack the clothing, learning materials, and support their schools need. Support of this kind would provide tailored clothing, educational supplies, and community activities for students at a special needs center.

Our Model

Where We Are Now

HOEP works on a simple model. A local partner organization identifies a specific person or group and defines the full cost of helping them. HOEP then raises that amount and sponsors that person or group directly. Nothing is funded from a general pool, and no program starts until the need and the full cost are clear.

Our founder will be in Kenya from November 30 to December 2, 2026, meeting partner organizations in person. Our first sponsored group will be chosen after that visit, and its story will be the first real story told on this page.
